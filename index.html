    Define

    Within 24 hours

The brief

A perfume house with a catalogue that runs from attar roll-ons and oudh to bakhoor, body mists and travel sets — and a brand that sells on atmosphere before it sells on price.

The problem

Eight distinct collections, each with its own customer. A store that had to feel luxurious without feeling slow. And the question every small store gets by email: where is my order?

The approach

  1. 01

    Open on atmosphere

    The homepage opens on a full-bleed product video. Price comes second; the feeling comes first.

  2. 02

    Make a big catalogue easy

    Eight collections, each with its own catalogue page, product search, and a catalogue customers can download.

  3. 03

    Answer the question before it’s asked

    Customers follow their order on a tracking page instead of emailing to ask where it is.

  4. 04

    Give customers a reason to return

    Customer accounts, sale pricing and combo packs, all running on the store’s own checkout.

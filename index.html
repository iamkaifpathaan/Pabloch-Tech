Questions

The things people ask first.

Do I need to buy a domain first?

No. If you already own one — even one that's just sitting on a parking page — I'll point it at the new site, which takes about five minutes. If you don't have one, I'll help you pick and register it. It usually runs $10–15 a year and it stays registered in your name, not mine.

How long does it take?

The free first draft is usually with you within a few days. After that, a small site is typically live in one to two weeks, and a storefront in three to five — but the real variable is how quickly you can get me your photos, prices and opening hours. That's almost always the slow part, not the code.

What does the monthly fee actually cover?

Hosting, SSL and your domain kept pointed and renewed. Your enquiry forms monitored, so you find out if something stops arriving. New reviews, price changes, seasonal updates and small edits — you email me, I do it, no hourly charge. If nothing needs doing that month, nothing needs doing; you're paying for the site staying live, current and looked after.

Can I cancel? What happens to my site?

Cancel any time with 30 days' notice. The site is yours — I hand over the files and you can host them anywhere, or I'll help you move them. The domain has always been in your name. Nothing is held hostage.

What if I already have a website?

Send me the link. Sometimes the honest answer is that it's fine and you'd be better off spending the money elsewhere — I'll tell you if that's the case. If it's slow, unreadable on a phone, or hasn't been touched since 2019, a rebuild is usually cheaper than trying to repair it.

Why is the first draft free?

Because describing a website is a waste of both our time when I could just show you one. It's the fastest way for you to judge whether I'm any good, and the fastest way for me to find out whether we'd work well together. If you don't like it, you owe me nothing and we shake hands.

Can we get on a call first?

Yes, and it's usually the fastest way to work out whether this is worth doing. Zoom or Google Meet, whichever you already have — Meet needs nothing installed, it opens in a browser tab. Twenty minutes, I'll ask what the business actually needs and tell you honestly if a website isn't it. Pick a platform in the form above and I'll send the invite with my reply. Happy to do it on Telegram or an ordinary phone call instead if you'd rather.

How do I pay from outside India?

Bank transfer, Wise, Payoneer or PayPal, invoiced in USD, GBP, AED or INR — whichever is easiest for you. You get a proper invoice for your records, and there's no minimum contract beyond the 30 days' notice on the care plan.

Will it show up on Google?

Every site is built with clean markup, proper page titles and descriptions, and structured data so Google understands what the business is and where it operates. That's the foundation, and it's included. Ranking above established competitors for competitive terms is a longer game that depends on reviews, listings and content — I'll be straight with you about what's realistic rather than promising page one.

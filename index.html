Honest answers

Still wondering if you need a website?

The six things people actually say when we bring this up. No sales pitch — just what we'd tell you on a call.

I already have Instagram.

Instagram is rented ground, and it's built for people who already follow you. Someone searching “decorator near me” at nine at night doesn't find your grid — and even if they do, they can't see your prices, your areas or your reviews in one place.

My business is too small.

Every business on this page is one person or a small crew. Small is exactly who this is for — a site levels the field against the bigger firm with a marketing budget and worse reviews than yours.

I don't know what I need.

Then don't decide yet. Tell us what the business does and we'll come back with what we'd build and what it would cost. Working that out is our job, not yours.

Websites are expensive.

Agencies are expensive. A site here starts at $400, the price is fixed in writing before work starts, and you see the draft first. If one extra customer a year covers it, the maths is not complicated.

I don't want to deal with developers.

There's no account manager, no ticket number and no portal to log into. You email or message one person, and that person makes the change. Plain English, always.

I don't know if I'll like the design.

Which is the whole reason the first draft is free. You'll be looking at a real, working version of your site before you've spent anything — and if it's not right, you walk away owing nothing.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Is the first website draft really free?

Yes, and there's no catch worth hunting for. You tell us about the business, we build a working first draft, and we send you a link. No deposit, no card details, no contract, no obligation to continue. You only pay if you like the direction and want us to build the real thing.

Why we do it: describing a website is a waste of both our time when we could just show you one. It's the fastest way for you to judge whether we're any good, and the fastest way for us to find out whether we'd work well together.

What happens if I don't like the draft?

Nothing. You owe us nothing, you're not billed for the time, and we don't chase you. Tell us it's not right and that's the end of it — no hard feelings.

If it's close but not there, say so. Often one conversation about what's off is enough to fix it, and that costs nothing either.

Is the free draft a complete website?

No, and we'd rather be upfront about that than have you find out later. The draft is a real, working page — genuine layout, your services, your words, opens on your phone, you can click around it. It is not a picture or a template with your logo dropped on top.

What it doesn't include yet: final copy and photography, working forms wired to your inbox, integrations, payment setup, full SEO work, testing across devices, your domain, and launch. That's the production build, and it starts once you approve the direction.

How much does a website cost?

Three starting points. Essential from $400 for a clean professional presence of around five pages. Growth from $700 for eight to ten pages with lead-generation features, integrations and analytics. Storefront from $1,200 for a complete online store with cart, checkout, payment gateway and order management.

You get a fixed figure in writing before any work starts, so the number you agree is the number you pay.

Why does the price say “starting at”?

Because a five-page site for a decorator and a store with four hundred products are not the same job, and pretending otherwise would mean either overcharging you or quietly cutting corners.

What moves the number: how many pages or products, which integrations and third-party services you need, custom functionality, and how much content we're writing versus you supplying. We'll tell you where you land before you commit — and if your requirements are simple, you pay the starting price.

Do I own my website?

Completely. The site, the code and the domain are yours. The domain is registered in your name from day one, and on request we hand over the files so you can host them anywhere or take them to another developer.

Nothing is locked to us and nothing is held hostage. That's deliberate — we'd rather you stay because the work is good.

Do you use WordPress or website builders?

No. Every site is written by hand in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with a real back end where a store needs one. No Wix, no Squarespace, no WordPress theme carrying forty plugins.

That matters for three practical reasons: pages load in well under a second, there's no plugin to break itself during an update at 3am, and there's no monthly platform fee you're stuck paying to keep your own site online.

Can you build an online store?

Yes — that's the Storefront package, from $1,200. Product catalogue and collections, cart, checkout, payment gateway integration, order management, customer order confirmations, basic shipping and tax configuration, search and filters.

Al-Abuzer Perfumes in the work section above is a live example: eight collections, cart, checkout, customer accounts and order tracking, in production and taking orders.

Can you integrate payments?

Yes. Payment gateway integration is included in the Storefront starting package.

One honest caveat: which gateway you can use, and how quickly it goes live, depends on the provider's own requirements — your country, your business documents and their approval process, none of which we control. We'll integrate whichever gateway you're approved for and tell you early if something looks likely to hold you up.

Do you provide hosting and a domain?

We set both up and get everything live, but they're registered in your name and paid for by you, so you're never locked in. A domain is usually around $10–15 a year and hosting is modest; we'll recommend what fits and walk you through it.

Already own a domain — even one sitting on a parking page? Even better. Pointing it at the new site is a five-minute change.

How long does a website take?

The free first draft is usually with you within a few days. After that, a small site is typically live in one to two weeks and a store in three to five, depending on complexity.

The honest variable is you. The slow part is almost never the code — it's waiting on photos, prices, opening hours and approvals. Get those to us quickly and the timeline holds.

How many revisions do I get?

Two full revision rounds are included in every package. A round means you go through the whole site and send back everything you want changed at once, and we work through the list.

In practice we're not precious about it — small tweaks after that don't get invoiced. What the limit is really there for is a third redesign in a different direction, which is a different job and gets quoted as one.

What happens after I approve the draft?

Five steps, in this order. 1. We send a one-page scope: what's included, the fixed price, the timeline. 2. You pay a 50% deposit and send your content — photos, prices, hours. 3. We build the production site: real forms, integrations, SEO, testing. 4. Two rounds of revisions. 5. Final 50% and we launch on your domain.

The timeline starts when the deposit and your content arrive, not when you say yes.

Do you provide maintenance after launch?

Yes — Website Care is optional and quoted per site. It covers hosting, SSL and your domain kept pointed and renewed, your enquiry forms monitored so nothing goes quietly missing, new reviews and prices and photos added as they come, and small edits without an hourly charge.

You're not obliged to take it. If you'd rather manage the site yourself, we hand it over and that's genuinely fine.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes, and the free draft works exactly the same way — we'll build a new version of your homepage so you can compare them side by side before deciding.

Sometimes the honest answer is that your current site is fine and you'd be better off spending the money elsewhere. We'll tell you if that's the case. But if it's slow, unreadable on a phone, or hasn't been touched since 2019, a rebuild is usually cheaper than repairing it.

Can you build custom functionality?

Usually, yes. Booking systems, price calculators, photo-upload quote forms, customer portals, membership areas, custom dashboards — the estimator and photo-upload forms in the work section above were all built from scratch for those specific businesses.

Custom work is quoted on top of the package price, based on what it actually involves. Describe what you want it to do and we'll tell you whether it's a small addition or a project of its own — and if it isn't something we should be building, we'll say that too.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Most of our work is outside India. We work US and UK business hours, and the projects on this page are in the United States, the United Kingdom and the UAE.

Invoicing is in USD, GBP, AED or INR by bank transfer, Wise, Payoneer or PayPal, and you get a proper invoice for your records. Time zones have never been the hard part — messages sent overnight are answered before you're back at your desk.

We'll reply within 24 hours with what we'd build, what it would cost and how long it would take. If it's a fit, the free draft follows. No deposit at any point in that process.
